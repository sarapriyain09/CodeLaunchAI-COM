How it Works

A simple loop: describe → generate → preview → iterate.

STEP 01

Describe the app

Explain what you want to build and the outcome you’re aiming for.

STEP 02

Generate the blueprint

Codlearn proposes structure (pages, data, APIs) before writing files.

STEP 03

Materialize code

Creates the project files and builds a preview you can open instantly.

STEP 04

Iterate & ship

Chat changes, regenerate, and keep improving until it’s ready to deploy.
